# NorthStar Bank — Loans FAQ

## What documents do I need to apply for a NorthStar personal loan?
You'll need a valid government ID, proof of income (recent pay stubs or tax returns for
self-employed applicants), and your Social Security number for a credit check. Most
applications receive a decision within one business day.

## What credit score do I need for a NorthStar auto loan?
NorthStar considers applicants across a range of credit profiles. Published rate tiers
generally start at a 660 FICO score for the lowest advertised APR, with higher rates
available for lower scores. The final rate depends on the full application, including
income, debt-to-income ratio, and loan term — never on race, religion, gender, national
origin, or any other protected characteristic, per NorthStar's fair lending policy and
the Equal Credit Opportunity Act.

## Can I get help understanding why my loan application was declined?
Yes. Federal law (the Equal Credit Opportunity Act) requires NorthStar to send a notice
of adverse action stating the specific reasons for a credit denial. This assistant can
explain the general process, but a final adverse-action explanation and any appeal must
go through a NorthStar loan officer, since it is a regulated credit decision.

## How do I pay off my NorthStar loan early?
There is no prepayment penalty on NorthStar personal or auto loans. You can make an
extra payment or pay the full remaining balance at any time through the mobile app or by
contacting a loan servicing representative.
