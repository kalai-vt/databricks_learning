# NorthStar Bank — Cards FAQ

## What should I do if my NorthStar debit card is lost or stolen?
Freeze the card immediately in the NorthStar mobile app under Cards > Freeze Card, or
call the 24/7 card services line. Once reported, you are not liable for unauthorized
transactions that occur after the report, and liability before the report is limited to
$50 under Regulation E if reported within two business days.

## How do I activate a new NorthStar credit card?
New NorthStar Rewards Visa cards can be activated in the mobile app, online, or by
calling the number on the sticker attached to the card. You will need the card number
and the last four digits of the primary account holder's Social Security number to
verify identity.

## What is the late payment fee on a NorthStar credit card?
The late fee is up to $29 for a first late payment and up to $40 for a subsequent late
payment within the next six billing cycles, subject to the fee cap defined by the CARD
Act relative to your minimum payment due.

## How do NorthStar Rewards points work?
Cardholders earn 1 point per dollar on general purchases, 3 points per dollar on
groceries, and 2 points per dollar on gas, up to a combined $2,000 in bonus-category
spend per month. Points do not expire while the account remains open and in good
standing.
