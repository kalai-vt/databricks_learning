# NorthStar Bank — Accounts FAQ

## What are the monthly fees on a NorthStar checking account?
The NorthStar Everyday Checking account has a monthly maintenance fee of $8. The fee is
waived if you maintain a minimum daily balance of $1,500, or if you receive a direct
deposit of at least $500 per month. NorthStar Student Checking has no monthly fee for
customers enrolled full-time and under age 24.

## What is the overdraft fee?
NorthStar charges a $30 overdraft fee per transaction, capped at three fees per day
($90 maximum). Customers can enroll in NorthStar Overdraft Shield, which links a savings
account to cover shortfalls with no fee, transferring in $50 increments.

## How do I open a NorthStar savings account?
You can open a NorthStar High-Yield Savings account online, by phone, or at any branch.
You will need a government-issued photo ID, your Social Security number, and an opening
deposit of at least $25. The current annual percentage yield (APY) is published on the
NorthStar rates page and updated monthly.

## How long do deposits take to clear?
Mobile check deposits made before 9 PM local time are typically available the next
business day, up to $2,500. Amounts above that may be held for up to 2 additional
business days per NorthStar's funds availability policy.

## How do I dispute a transaction on my account?
Contact NorthStar customer support within 60 days of the statement date. A specialist
will open a case, issue provisional credit within 10 business days where applicable, and
investigate per Regulation E requirements for electronic transfers.
