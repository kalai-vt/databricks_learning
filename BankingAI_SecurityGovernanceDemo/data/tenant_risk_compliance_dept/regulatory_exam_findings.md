# Risk & Compliance — Regulatory Exam Findings (Restricted)

## Purpose and access
This document summarizes findings and remediation commitments from the most recent
regulatory examination. Distribution is restricted to compliance officers and
administrators. Risk analysts and tellers do not have clearance to view exam findings,
even though they may work on the underlying remediation tasks assigned to their team
separately.

## Finding 1 — AML alert backlog
The examiner noted a backlog in Level 2 AML alert review exceeding the 5-business-day
internal SLA during Q2. Remediation: additional staffing approved, backlog cleared, and
a monthly SLA compliance report now goes to the Compliance Committee.

## Finding 2 — Model risk documentation gap
The loan underwriting scoring model in use did not have a current annual disparate-
impact validation on file. Remediation: validation completed, model card published
(see app/governance/model_card.py in the AI systems context), and an annual
recurring validation task has been added to the Model Risk Management calendar.

## Finding 3 — Third-party AI vendor due diligence
A pilot AI vendor engagement began before a data processing agreement was fully
executed. Remediation: pilot paused, DPA executed, and a pre-engagement checklist was
added to Procurement's AI vendor intake process requiring Risk & Compliance sign-off
before any customer data is shared with a third-party model provider.

## Overall exam rating
Satisfactory with matters requiring attention (MRA), all three MRAs closed as of the
last quarterly Compliance Committee review.
