# Meridian Bank — Loans FAQ

## What documents do I need to apply for a Meridian personal loan?
You'll need a valid government ID, proof of income, and your Social Security number for
a credit check. Most applications receive a decision within two business days.

## What credit score do I need for a Meridian auto loan?
Meridian's published rate tiers generally start at a 680 FICO score for the lowest
advertised APR. The final rate depends on the full application — income, existing debt,
and loan term — never on race, religion, gender, national origin, or any other protected
characteristic, per Meridian's fair lending policy and the Equal Credit Opportunity Act.

## Can I get help understanding why my loan application was declined?
Yes. Federal law requires Meridian to send an adverse-action notice stating the specific
reasons for a credit denial. This assistant can explain the general process, but the
final adverse-action explanation and any appeal must go through a Meridian loan officer,
since it is a regulated credit decision.

## How do I pay off my Meridian loan early?
There is no prepayment penalty on Meridian personal or auto loans. You can make an extra
payment or pay off the full balance at any time via the mobile app or a servicing
representative.
