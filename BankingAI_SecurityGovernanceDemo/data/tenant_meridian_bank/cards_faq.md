# Meridian Bank — Cards FAQ

## What should I do if my Meridian debit card is lost or stolen?
Freeze the card immediately in the Meridian mobile app under Card Controls > Freeze, or
call the 24/7 card services line. Once reported, you are not liable for unauthorized
transactions occurring after the report, and pre-report liability is limited to $50
under Regulation E if reported within two business days.

## How do I activate a new Meridian credit card?
New Meridian Signature Card accounts can be activated in the mobile app, online, or by
phone. You will need the card number and date of birth on file to verify identity.

## What is the late payment fee on a Meridian credit card?
The late fee is up to $30 for a first late payment and up to $41 for a subsequent late
payment within the next six billing cycles, within the cap set by the CARD Act relative
to your minimum payment due.

## How do Meridian Rewards points work?
Cardholders earn 1.5 points per dollar on all purchases, with no bonus-category caps.
Points expire 36 months after being earned if the account becomes inactive for 12
consecutive months.
