# Meridian Bank — Accounts FAQ

## What are the monthly fees on a Meridian checking account?
The Meridian Essential Checking account has a monthly maintenance fee of $12. The fee is
waived if you maintain a minimum daily balance of $2,000, or if you set up at least two
recurring direct deposits per month. Meridian has no student checking product.

## What is the overdraft fee?
Meridian charges a $34 overdraft fee per transaction, capped at four fees per day
($136 maximum). Meridian Balance Assist gives eligible customers a 24-hour grace period
to cover a shortfall before any fee is charged.

## How do I open a Meridian savings account?
Meridian Growth Savings can be opened online or at a branch with a government-issued
photo ID, your Social Security number, and a $100 minimum opening deposit. The current
APY is published on the Meridian rates page and updated monthly.

## How long do deposits take to clear?
Mobile check deposits made before 8 PM local time are typically available the next
business day, up to $2,000. Larger amounts may be held for up to 3 additional business
days under Meridian's funds availability policy.

## How do I dispute a transaction on my account?
Contact Meridian customer support within 60 days of the statement date to open a
dispute case. Provisional credit is issued within 10 business days where applicable,
consistent with Regulation E for electronic transfers.
